Weatherie

Short description 78/80

The weather, drawn eleven different ways. Pick the art you want to wake up to.

Full description 572/4000

Weatherie shows you the forecast inside a piece of generated art that changes with the sky — clear mornings, overcast afternoons, rain at night, each one its own painting.

Eleven themes, from cut-paper landscapes and stained glass to a small brass automaton alone in vast weather. Three are free. Every one carries around a hundred scenes, so the picture rarely repeats.

Everything you'd actually check is on one screen: hourly and seven-day forecasts, wind with a Beaufort dial, UV, humidity, sunrise and moon phase. Home-screen widgets in two sizes. No account needed.
